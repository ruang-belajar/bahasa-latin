> Ad maiorem Dei gloriam

💠 *For the glory of God*

- It serves as the official motto of the [Society of Jesus](https://www.jesuits.org/about-us/glossary/) (the Jesuits), a religious order within the Catholic Church. [[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad_maiorem_Dei_gloriam)]
- The phrase was popularized in the 16th century by [St. Ignatius of Loyola](https://yayasankanisius.sch.id/gallery/91/show), the founder of the Jesuits, as a guiding principle for life and work. [[1](https://yayasankanisius.sch.id/gallery/91/show)]. In Ignatian spirituality, it means that any action, choice, or daily task—no matter how small—can be done to praise and honor God

> ex tenebris lux

💠 *emerging light from a dark place*.

- It is a variation of _lux in tenebris_ ("light in darkness"), a biblical concept from the Gospel of John (_et lux in tenebris lucet_).
- People use this phrase to describe hope, knowledge, or clarity appearing after a difficult or ignorant time.
